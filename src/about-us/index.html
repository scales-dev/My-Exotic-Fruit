FAQ

Feel free to contact us if you have any questions!

What do you sell?

On myexoticfruit.com you will find a huge selection of high-quality elusive exotic fruits, sourced from all over the globe. Probably the largest collection of tropical fruits found on any website in the world! Simply order online and the fruits will be delivered to your door. Trust My Exotic Fruit, the UK's leading exotic fruit retailer, for high-quality, fresh produce and unbeatable variety.

How long will my order take to arrive?

Our aim is to dispatch orders within 3 working days of payment being received. Occasionally this is not possible due to the nature of our rare fresh produce.

Can I ask a question about a particular fruit?

We are more than happy to answer your questions. Our staff are very knowledgeable and have travelled the world in pursuit of exotic fruits so we should be able to answer your question. One of our employees has now tasted over 400 fruits – if it's worth having, he's had it! Almost, there are always new ones to discover. Next on his list, Peanut Butter Fruit! Simply email your question to info@myexoticfruit.com or call someone in the warehouse 01277 809810.

Is the fruit organic?

We try to source organically wherever we can. In order to ensure a reliable and consistent supply of fruits we have to work with many different suppliers, all of whom use different farms to procure their stock. This means that it's quite possible that one day we might, for example, have organic mangosteen but the following day we may have received mangosteen from a farm that doesn't farm organically. For up to date information about which fruits are organic, please contact us prior to placing your order.
